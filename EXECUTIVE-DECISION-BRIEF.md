## Constructed miniature example

Northbridge proposes a reservation capability reusable by a partner portal and
an approved service assistant. Leadership chooses `PROCEED BOUNDED` for one
partner and one reservation type. The scenario benefit is fewer manual handoffs;
it is not measured. Expansion requires evidence that consumers distinguish
accepted from completed reservations, duplicates do not create additional
holds, unknown outcomes reconcile, and support can reconstruct each result.
This example is constructed teaching material, not a John Briggs project or an
observed business result.
