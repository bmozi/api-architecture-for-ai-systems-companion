# API Value and Evidence Ledger

Use this ledger to connect a proposed capability to value without converting a
forecast, completed template, or successful demonstration into measured ROI.

**Status:** Blank development template; usability untested

## Plain-language fields

- **Beneficiary:** The person or group expected to receive value.
- **Current burden:** A delay, workaround, cost, error, or missed opportunity.
- **Proposed capability:** The useful outcome the API would expose.
- **Commitment:** What the organization must build, operate, secure, support,
  govern, and evolve.
- **Leading evidence:** An early observable signal that the capability is being
  used and behaving as intended.
- **Outcome evidence:** Evidence tied to the beneficiary's result.
- **Counterevidence:** A result that weakens the value or architecture case.
- **Reconsideration trigger:** A condition that forces a new decision.

## Evidence check

For every number, mark one:

`MEASURED | REPORTED | ESTIMATED | SCENARIO | UNKNOWN`

Record the population, window, source, assumptions, and exclusions. Do not add
different evidence states into one confident total.

## Outside-team test

Ask a person outside the provider team to explain why the capability matters,
what it commits the organization to operate, and which result would stop the
investment. Record disagreement instead of rewriting their answer as agreement.
