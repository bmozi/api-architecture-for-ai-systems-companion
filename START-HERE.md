# Start Here: One Capability in Thirty Minutes

**Reader route:** Complete the thirty-minute exercise output first. The
facilitator-only freeze, manifest, and evidence protocol is a separate route
for controlled pilots; do not open it until the first-pass artifact is done.

**Plain language:** Delegated authority is the specific power a caller may use,
who granted it, and where the system enforces the limit.

Choose one useful action a person, partner, application, automation, or AI tool
needs from an existing system. Do not begin with the endpoint.

## 0–5 minutes: say why it matters

Write one sentence for each:

1. The person or system needs to ___;
2. because today ___;
3. and a trustworthy reusable capability would unlock ___.

If the answer is “access the database” or “call the service,” move one level
up. Name the business outcome.

## 5–15 minutes: define the promise

Complete only the [five-sentence first pass](api-meaning-and-authority-brief.md#ten-minute-first-pass)
in the API Meaning-and-Authority Brief. Compare it with the
[miniature Northbridge example](api-meaning-and-authority-brief.md#miniature-example).

Stop if accepted and completed mean the same thing only by assumption, or if
the party granting authority cannot be named.

## 15–25 minutes: separate the roles

Use the [interaction decision guide](api-event-workflow-decision-tree.md) to ask:

- Is the consumer requesting a capability?
- Does an AI client need an MCP-facing description of that approved capability?
- Must another party learn that a fact occurred?
- Does responsibility remain open across time?
- Is any bounded decision being delegated?

Composition is allowed. Vagueness is not.

## 25–30 minutes: ask for a read-back

Give the five sentences to someone outside the producing team. Ask what the
consumer may request, what success means, what happens after an unknown result,
and who owns the promise. Record disagreement as design work.

## Your first result

You now have a reviewable capability hypothesis, not an approved API. Continue
with the [comprehensive example](examples/northbridge-inventory-reservation-meaning-authority-example.md),
then select the authority, recovery, compatibility, capacity, and evidence
tools required by the consequence.
