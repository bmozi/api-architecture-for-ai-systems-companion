## Supplied answers versus blank tools

Each numbered chapter contains a supplied case and its worked answer. Several
links above lead to blank decision tools, not completed answers; use them to
record your transfer after attempting the chapter case. For a second domain,
use the [equipment-checkout exercise](supplied-practice-route.md).

## How to read an answer

An answer is annotated when it identifies the outcome, authority, boundary,
failure behavior, evidence status, and reversal or stopping trigger—not merely
when it fills every cell. Preserve `planned`, `unrun`, `unknown`, and negative
results. A complete form can still describe an unsafe design.
