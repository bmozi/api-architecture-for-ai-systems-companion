# Consent and Privacy Boundaries

## Purpose

You are being invited to try two architecture worksheets against a fictional
system-design scenario. The purpose is to find unclear prompts, missing
decisions, and unsafe interpretations in the worksheets. This is a test of the
materials, not a test of you or your employer.

The session is not a job evaluation, certification examination, security
assessment, legal review, or claim that the resulting design is production
ready. Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop at any time
without giving a reason.

## Fiction and confidentiality

Cedarway Home Services, HomePort, RouteSpring, and all described systems and
incidents are fictional. Please reason only from the provided scenario.

Do not disclose:

- employer, customer, employee, or vendor names;
- confidential architecture, incidents, credentials, contracts, or metrics;
- personal data about customers or coworkers; or
- security weaknesses that identify a real system.

You may say that a prompt reminds you of a general pattern, but keep examples
abstract. The facilitator should interrupt and redirect if identifiable or
confidential information enters the discussion.

## Information collected

The proposed minimal record is:

- a participant code rather than your name;
- optional broad role and experience bands;
- your completed worksheets and workbook;
- elapsed time by activity;
- questions, pauses, revisions, and skipped fields;
- short quotations when they explain an interpretation;
- facilitator prompts and interventions; and
- an assessment of the worksheets, including negative results.

Audio, video, screen recording, names, contact details, and employer identity
are outside the default test record. Any recording requires separate, explicit
consent and a stated retention plan before the session begins.
