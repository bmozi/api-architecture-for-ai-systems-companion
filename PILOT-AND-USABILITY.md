## Pilot question

Can representative readers explain why API architecture matters after AI,
complete a useful capability brief, detect a consequential failure, transfer
the decision across roles, and identify evidence for a next decision?

## Intended participant mix

Recruit across developer, architect, manager, product/operations, security, and
executive decision-owner perspectives. Record actual roles and experience; do
not infer representativeness from job title alone.

## Observable gates

1. recognizes the problem before specialist terminology;
2. explains the capability promise in plain language;
3. completes a first artifact without invented authority;
4. supports an outside-team read-back;
5. detects or discusses seeded failure;
6. produces a team decision with evidence, an assigned owner or explicit
   `UNASSIGNED` state plus assignment authority/trigger, and a justified date or
   evidence-based review trigger; and
7. makes an executive decision without promoting scenario benefit to measured
   ROI.
