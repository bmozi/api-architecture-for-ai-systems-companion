  Meaning-and-Authority Brief to the recurring composite.

  Error-to-Recovery Map, Idempotency and Outcome Test Matrix, and Compatibility

  Authority and Delegation Map, Contract Evidence Mutation Ledger, and Outcome
  Evidence Map to the same frozen reservation and review scenario. Every test,
  mutation, and reconstruction result is `unrun`; the example is a coherent
  tool application, not empirical evidence.
- `northbridge-collection-bulk-and-capacity-example.md` applies the Collection
  and Bulk Decision Record and Capacity and Exhaustion Matrix. Every mutation,
  load, recovery, abuse, and degradation result remains `unrun`.
